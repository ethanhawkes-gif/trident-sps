Our Story

A father-son operation that earns the business every day.

Bruce and Connor Hannough launched Trident in 2019 — months before COVID tore the supply chain apart. While the industry seized up, they kept food processors and cold-chain operators supplied. That trial set the standard they still run by.

Bruce leads sales and client relationships with 30-plus years in perishable protein. Connor runs operations, logistics, and the infrastructure that keeps 65+ accounts moving across the country.

"Build something durable, stay close to your clients, and never stop earning the business."— The Trident operating principle

Sustainable by Design

Reusable isn't a feature. It's the whole point.

Based in a fishing community, we've seen what disposable packaging does to the water. Every container we put into service is engineered to last for years — and when it does reach end of life, we work to recycle it responsibly, keeping plastic out of landfills and out of the water.

Common Questions

Straight answers.

How quickly will I get a quote?

Within one business day. Bruce personally reviews quote requests, walks through your application, and specs the right container for your line — no call center, no runaround.

Are your containers food-grade?

Yes. Our containers are manufactured from food-grade, FDA-compliant resins and are specified for use in USDA-compliant processing environments. Spec sheets are available on request for anything in the catalog.

Do you carry stock, or is everything made to order?

Both. We maintain live stock you can check any time, and we source and spec to order for applications that need something specific.

What industries do you serve?

Seafood, pork and poultry processing, distribution, and cold chain. We serve 65+ accounts across the country, including PepsiCo, Perdue Farms, Olymel, Darling Ingredients, and Northern Pelagic Group.

What happens to containers at the end of their life?

We work to recycle them responsibly. But the first answer is that they last: every container we put into service is engineered to be reused for years before that conversation ever comes up.

Where are you located, and do you ship nationwide?

Kittery Point, Maine. Trident SPS was founded in 2019 by Bruce and Connor Hannough, a father and son, and we ship to accounts across the United States.
